What StockWise analyses for you

Two kinds of analysis, one simple forecast.

1 · Fundamental analysis

Is it a good business at a fair price?

Company ratios are downloaded automatically and rated so you instantly see strengths and weaknesses.

ROE

Return on Equity — profit earned on shareholders' money.

ROIC

Return on Invested Capital — profit earned on debt + equity.

Net Profit Margin

How much profit is kept from every ₹100 of sales.

P/E Ratio

Price you pay for ₹1 of yearly earnings.

P/B Ratio

Price compared to the company's net assets.

PEG Ratio

P/E adjusted for how fast earnings grow.

2 · Technical analysis

What are price and volume saying?

Seven signals are scored from −1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish) and combined with fixed weights you can see on every stock page.

Moving Averages

SMA 20 and SMA 50 show the trend direction.

MACD

Momentum: is the trend speeding up or slowing down?

RSI

Overbought above 70, oversold below 30.

Bollinger Bands

Volatility bands around the 20-day average.

BIAS

How far (%) price has stretched from its average.

Order-flow pressure

Estimated buying vs selling volume (order-book proxy).

Volume Footprint

Volume-at-price profile with the Point of Control (POC) and 70% value area for the last 60 days.

3 · Next-week outlook

A simple, transparent forecast

We combine 75% technical score and 25% fundamental score, then use the stock's normal weekly volatility to draw an expected price range for the next 5 trading days.

Every stock page also shows a back-test hit rate: how often the technical signal's direction matched the real move over the past year. Around 50% means the signal is no better than a coin flip for that stock — that honesty helps you decide how much weight to give it.

AI news sentiment: headlines are read by FinBERT, a deep learning (BERT) model trained on financial text, and tagged Positive / Neutral / Negative. The average gives each stock and sector a "news mood".
