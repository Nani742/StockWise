How to use StockWise

A quick start, a glossary of every number you'll see, and answers to common questions.

Quick start

1
Create an account

Click Register at the top right.

2
Search a stock

On the Dashboard type e.g. TCS, choose NSE, click Analyse.

3
Add to watchlist

Your watchlist shows a small chart and next-week range for each stock.

4
Decide yourself

Read the signals, do your own research, then decide.

Glossary

Net profit ÷ shareholders' equity. 15%+ is generally strong. Very high ROE with lots of debt can be misleading — check ROIC too.

After-tax operating profit ÷ (debt + equity − cash). Shows how well the business turns all its money into profit. 12%+ is good.

Net profit ÷ revenue. A 15% margin means ₹15 profit from every ₹100 of sales. Compare with companies in the same industry.

P/E = price ÷ earnings per share. P/B = price ÷ book value per share. PEG = P/E ÷ earnings growth %. Lower usually means cheaper, but cheap stocks can be cheap for a reason.

Average closing price of the last 20 / 50 days. Price above both, with SMA20 above SMA50, is a classic uptrend.

Difference between the 12-day and 26-day exponential averages, compared with its own 9-day average (signal line). Green growing histogram = rising momentum.

Relative Strength Index, 0–100. Above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold. Strong trends can stay overbought for a long time.

Bollinger Bands sit 2 standard deviations above/below the 20-day average. BIAS is the % distance of price from that average. Both show how stretched the price is.

A real order book shows every pending buy/sell order and needs a paid live broker feed (e.g. Zerodha Kite Connect, Upstox API). StockWise estimates buy vs sell pressure from daily data: when a day closes near its high, most volume is counted as buying.

We add up how much volume traded at each price level over 60 days. The level with the most volume is the Point of Control (POC); the zone holding 70% of volume is the value area. True tick-by-tick footprint charts need intraday tick data.

FAQ

How accurate is the forecast?

Nobody can predict markets reliably. Each stock page shows the past hit rate of the signal — often close to 50–60%. Treat it as one input, not an answer.

Why does a ratio show N/A?

Yahoo Finance doesn't publish every ratio for every company (e.g. banks often lack ROIC). N/A ratios are simply left out of the score.

What does "DEMO DATA" mean?

The app is running on generated practice prices (offline mode). Those numbers are not real — never use them for decisions.

How fresh is the data?

Daily prices from Yahoo Finance, which may be delayed. Results are reused for 15 minutes to keep the app fast.

What is "News mood"?

An AI model (FinBERT) reads each headline and labels it Positive, Neutral or Negative. News mood is the average. It's the AI's reading of headlines, can be wrong, and is not a buy/sell signal.

Why isn't news mood part of the forecast?

We only mix signals into the forecast that we can back-test on past data. Old headlines aren't available for free, so news mood is shown separately and saved daily to build that history.
