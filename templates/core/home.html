About StockWise

Research made simple for everyday investors

Most beginners buy stocks because of tips from friends, social media or TV. StockWise helps you look at the facts instead: how profitable the company is, whether the price is expensive, and what the chart is saying right now.

  • Plain-English explanations for every number.
  • Small charts for every stock in your watchlist with a next-week range.
  • Honest accuracy: we show how often the signal was right in the past.
Features

Everything you need to analyse a stock

Fundamentals

ROE, ROIC, Net Profit Margin, P/E, P/B and PEG rated Good / Okay / Weak.

Technicals

Moving averages, MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands and BIAS with bullish / bearish signals.

Volume insight

Order-flow pressure and a volume footprint (Point of Control) to see where buyers defend.

Next-week outlook

Likely Up / Down / Sideways with an expected price range for the next 5 trading days.
